A5 comparator mirror

Four plates, drawn to the house clear-line rules: one uniform stroke weight, flat colour, no shading, amber reserved for the mirror face and the angles that matter.

One reading to confirm: I took “the notebook is the wall” literally, so the notebook stands and the leaf projects out over the page. I also read “at a 45° angle” as the rod’s angle, with the mirror square to the rod so its face looks straight back at the leaf. Say the word and I’ll redraw with the rod hanging plumb and the mirror alone tilted 45°.
